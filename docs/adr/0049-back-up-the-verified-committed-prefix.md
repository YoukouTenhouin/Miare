# Back up the verified committed prefix

`backupTo()` holds the writer lane, verifies the reachable graph and allocator partition, and copies the selected generation's physical prefix through its committed high-water boundary without re-encryption, relocation, reclamation, or publication-slot normalization. Preserving identity, generations, positions, nonces, and free or retired representation makes backup an interoperable fidelity operation with no second format-building path; “clean” means self-contained and free of an abandoned tail or required sidecars, while users invoke compaction separately when physical size matters.
